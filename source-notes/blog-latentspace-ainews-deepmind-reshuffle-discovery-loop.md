# [AINews] Jeff, Sanjay, Oriol, and Quoc depart DeepMind; Demis to Chair; Koray to SVP — what is going on at GDM???

> Latent Space's AINews digest for August 6, 2026 (covering 8/4-8/5) leads
> with a coordinated departure of four senior Google DeepMind
> leaders — Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le — to found
> Discovery Loop, a Public Benefit Corporation explicitly targeting
> "autoresearch / automated discovery loops" over scientific and engineering
> workflows, alongside a simultaneous GDM leadership reshuffle (Demis
